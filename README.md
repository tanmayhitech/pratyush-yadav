# Pratyush Yadav — The Cinematic Monograph

An immersive, cinematic personal digital monograph for **Pratyush Yadav** based in **Lucknow, India**.

Designed with the atmosphere, restraint, and chiaroscuro lighting inspired by *The Godfather*.

---

## What’s New

1. **Pure Cinematic Experience**:
   - **No traditional navbar**: The experience opens directly with the dramatic title sequence: **PRATYUSH YADAV**.
   - **Chiaroscuro & Film Grain**: Subtle film grain overlay, deep atmospheric vignette, and cinematic lighting.
   - **Godfather Pacing**: Scene intermissions (*“Keep your friends close.”*, *“It’s not personal.”*), Roman numeral indices (`I`, `II`, `III`), and disciplined negative space.

2. **Noir Mode & Crimson Blood Mode**:
   - **NOIR Mode**: Pitch-black canvas (`#050505`), charcoal surfaces, off-white typography, and gold/crimson accents.
   - **CRIMSON Mode**: Deep burgundy/velvet black (`#080203`), glowing blood red accents (`#B91C1C` / `#DC2626`), and deep red cinematic chiaroscuro mood.
   - Instant theme toggle located in the discreet floating top-bar with `localStorage` memory.

3. **Content & Philosophy**:
   - Main Statement: *“The world is what I can make sense of. The rest, I leave unnamed...”*
   - Key Line: *“The mind that creates should never apologize.”*
   - **Perspectives Triptych**:
     - `I — Autonomy`: Personal Stance (discipline, self-governance, silence).
     - `II — The City`: Lucknow (gravity, Nawabi poise, dignity).
     - `III — Calculation`: Observation (measuring dynamics, strategic silence).
   - **Connect Monolith & Dispatch Modal**: Direct messaging modal with validation and *“Message received.”* confirmation.
   - **Fullscreen Portrait Lightbox**: Expandable portrait view with `Escape` key close.

## How to Add or Change the Portrait Photo

Drop your photo into `assets/` and update `index.html`:

```html
<!-- Hero section portrait -->
<img src="assets/your-photo.jpg" alt="Pratyush Yadav" class="portrait-texture-img" id="mainPortraitImg" />

<!-- Lightbox portrait -->
<img src="assets/your-photo.jpg" alt="Pratyush Yadav" class="lightbox-full-img" id="lightboxImg" />
```
